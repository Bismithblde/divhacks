# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js frontend and backend with Tailwind CSS. Mobile-first PWA.

## Users

People walking in NYC, using a map on mobile to get around obstacles. The first version is walking-first.

## Product Purpose

A DivHacks project offering a map-based alternative to Google Maps, with a focus on navigating around parades, protests, and road closures.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Planned core capability: route around disruptions. Initial coverage is NYC, walking-first. Map provider, routing provider, disruption data source, deployment target, and final product name remain undecided.

## Product Principles

- Prioritize mobile use.
- Make obstacles relevant to a journey visible.
- Help users find a route around disruptions.
